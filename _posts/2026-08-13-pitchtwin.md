date: 2026-08-13 00:00:00 +0900
---

Real-time 3D digital-twin of a football match, reconstructed from 2D broadcast video. Rebuilds the play as an interactive 3D replay: a tactical top-down view, a free-orbit camera, and later a first-person view from any player. The tracking feed is built from raw video with its own CV pipeline, so no proprietary league data is required.

**Tech:** Python (BoT-SORT + ReID, homography calibration, jersey OCR), Three.js / WebGL, Vite
